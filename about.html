Background

My story

I started my career in frontline technical support for a cybersecurity product – troubleshooting, understanding customers, and learning the technology from the ground up. That technical foundation became the backbone of everything that followed.

Over the next two decades, I progressed through web operations, revenue optimization, customer acquisition and retention, and online sales management – before building the entire marketing function at Heimdal from scratch as CMO.

Today I lead a full-stack marketing team spanning demand generation, product marketing, SEO/AEO, paid search, affiliate marketing, direct marketing, marketing automation, graphical design, and web development – all focused on driving pipeline and revenue in a highly competitive cybersecurity market.
